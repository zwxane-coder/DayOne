About DayOne

The world before you. The day you arrived.

DayOne rebuilds the world around one birthday — the weather, the songs, the prices and the headlines — using open data.

Why DayOne exists

Everyone knows their birthday. Almost no one knows what that day was like: whether it rained, what was playing on the radio, what a loaf of bread cost, or what the front page said.

DayOne answers those questions. Enter a date, add a name and a birthplace if you like, and it assembles a five-chapter story of the day you arrived — then lets you compare it with anyone else’s, explore any year since 1920, or turn it into a keepsake certificate.

What’s inside

🌤

The day itself

Weekday, season, moon phase, sunrise and sunset, plus the weather at your birthplace when records exist (from 1940).

♫

The culture

Chart-topping songs with cover art, the biggest films of your birth year, and famous people who share your birthday.

🪙

The world

A shopping receipt from your birth year next to today’s prices, plus headlines and country statistics from the time.

How it works

  1. Enter a birthday.A name and birthplace are optional, but a place unlocks weather and local data.
  2. Read your five chapters.Arrival, Sky, Soundtrack, Prices and Headlines — each pulled live from open sources.
  3. Share it.Compare two birthdays, browse any year in Explore, or create a certificate to download or print.

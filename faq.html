FAQ

Questions, answered.

The short answers to what people ask most. Can’t find yours? Get in touch.

What is DayOne?
DayOne reconstructs the world around a birthday: the weather, number-one songs, everyday prices, headlines and more. See About DayOne for the full story.
Do I need an account?
No. There is nothing to sign up for. Enter a birthday and your story opens straight away.
Why is the birthplace optional?
Weather, local charts, currency and country statistics all depend on where you were born. Without a place you still get a complete story using global data.
Why is there no weather for my birthday?
Weather records in DayOne begin in 1940, and a birthplace is needed to look them up. Add a place, or try a date from 1940 onward.
How accurate are the prices?
They are estimates. DayOne uses national United States benchmark prices for items like bread, milk and a movie ticket, then converts them using purchasing-power data when your birthplace has it. Every price is labelled as estimated.
Why does my soundtrack show a different year?
Ranked year-end charts start in 1946. For birthdays before 1950, My Day shows the earliest year-end chart on record. The Explore page goes back further with a selection of popular songs.
Where do the headlines come from?
From Wikipedia’s current-events pages and its “On this day” feed. Exact daily news logs are only available for some dates, so DayOne tells you when it is showing events from the same date in other years.
Is my information saved?
Your name, birthday and birthplace are not stored on a server. They travel in the page address so your story can be shared, and your theme choice is remembered in your own browser. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
How do I share my day or make a certificate?
Open Gift a Story, or use the button at the end of your My Day page. You can download the certificate as an image, print it, or copy a link to the full story.
What are the themes?
There are five: spring, summer, autumn, winter and tropical. Use the switch on the right edge of any page. Your choice is remembered. If you haven’t picked one, My Day follows the season of your birth, or tropical for birthplaces near the equator.
Something looks wrong or is missing. What do I do?
Send us the date and place using the contact form. Every chart and news item also has a source link you can follow.

Sources & Accuracy

Where every fact comes from.

DayOne reads open data and shows its working. Here is what each source supplies, and where the limits are.

How accurate is it?

  • Weather comes from the ERA5 reanalysis, which starts in 1940. It is modelled from observations, so it can differ from a local weather station.
  • Songs use Billboard’s year-end lists from 1946. Earlier years in Explore show a selection of popular songs because no ranked chart exists. For birthdays before 1950, My Day shows the earliest year-end chart on record.
  • Prices are national United States benchmarks, estimated between benchmark years. When a birthplace has purchasing-power data, they are converted to local currency and labelled as estimates.
  • Headlines for an exact day are only logged for some dates. Otherwise DayOne shows notable events on the same calendar date from other years, and says so.
  • Missing data is left out rather than guessed. If something looks wrong, tell us and include the date and place.

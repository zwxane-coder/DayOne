1Who we are and what this covers

DayOne is a website that rebuilds the world around a birthday: the weather, music, prices and news from a date. This policy explains what information the site handles, where it goes and what choices you have. It applies to every page on DayOne.

The short version: there are no accounts, we do not sell personal data, and your birthday is used in your browser to build your page.

2Information you give us

You only enter information when you use a feature. Nothing is required except a date.

Home and My DayDate of birth, plus an optional name and birthplace.
CompareTwo dates, optional names and birthplaces.
Gift a StoryA name, date, optional birthplace and an optional personal message for the certificate.
ContactWhatever you write to us, usually your name, email address and message.

3How we use it

  • To build your pages: your day, a comparison or a certificate.
  • To remember your choice of season theme on your device.
  • To reply when you contact us.
  • To keep the site working and secure.

We do not use your birthday to profile you, and we do not use it for advertising.

4Services that help build your page

DayOne draws on public data services. To answer your request, your browser sends each one only what it needs. Your name is not sent to any of them.

Open-MeteoThe place you type (to find coordinates) and, for weather, the coordinates and date.
Wikipedia and WikimediaCalendar dates and years, to fetch events, births, charts and science lists.
World Bank, REST CountriesA country code and year, for population, life expectancy and currency facts.
Apple iTunes SearchSong and artist names, to load album artwork.
Google FontsLoads our typefaces. Google receives your IP address and browser details when fonts load.

Each service has its own privacy policy. We do not control how they handle requests.

5Your details in the address bar

When you open My Day, Compare or a shared story link, your date, name and birthplace are written into the page address. That means they can appear in your browser history and bookmarks, and anyone you send the link to will see them. Share links only with people you are comfortable sharing that information with.

6Cookies and local storage

DayOne does not use advertising cookies. The site saves your chosen season theme in your browser’s local storage so it is the same next time. You can clear it at any time from your browser settings, and the site will keep working.

7How long information is kept

We do not store birthdays, names or birthplaces on our own servers. They stay in your browser, in the address bar and in your history, until you clear them. Messages sent through Contact are kept only as long as needed to reply and keep a record, and for no more than 12 months afterwards.

8Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, to object to or restrict how it is used, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because DayOne does not hold an account for you, most of this is in your hands: clear your history, delete shared links and do not submit details you do not want to share. For anything we hold, such as a Contact message, get in touch and we will help.

9Children

DayOne is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you enter a child’s birthday, for example for a gift, please make sure you are their parent or guardian or have their guardian’s permission.

10Security and international processing

DayOne is served over an encrypted connection where your host supports it, but no system is perfectly secure. The services in section 4 may process requests in other countries, so information in those requests can leave your own.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as DayOne changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant, we will say so on the home page.
